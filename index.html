The shop

Heroes of hospitality,
this is your sign.

Macgregor's Original is a smash burger shop in Abbotsford doing one thing really, really well: simple, American-style burgers with zero gimmicks and maximum flavour.

No stunt burgers. No towering nonsense dripping down your elbow. Just smashed patties, melty cheese, buttered Martin's rolls, and chips cooked in beef tallow the way the burger gods intended.

We're growing, and we need good humans on the grill, in the kitchen, and out front.

Previously on this site

Our shop used to be a movie set for a Hollywood film. Now it's the neighbourhood's favourite burger joint.

We think that's a pretty good career trajectory, and we'd like yours to follow a similar arc.
